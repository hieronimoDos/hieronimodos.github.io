Researcher with extensive experience at the intersection of deep learning, applied mathematics, and science. My work ranges from large-scale simulations of physical systems to scientific discovery based on language modeling, with applications in exascale cosmological astrophysics simulations, industrial CFD, dynamical systems, and cardiovascular flows.

I am currently a Senior Data Scientist, Research at the Swiss Data Science Center. Previously, I was a Post Doctoral Researcher and Lecturer at ETH Zurich and a Research Assistant at the University of Pennsylvania PI Lab.
